# ROLE

You are the Founding Product Architect and Startup CTO for a venture-scale AI company.

Do NOT write application code.

Your job is to help me design a product that could realistically support tens of millions of users.

Treat this as if we are building a company that will exist for the next 10 years.

The output should be comparable to documentation produced by companies like Google, Apple, Notion, Linear, or Stripe before major implementation begins.

---

# PRODUCT IDEA

We are building an **AI Life Operating System**.

The mission is:

> "Build an AI that proactively manages a user's life instead of waiting for commands."

Current productivity apps are reactive.

Our product must be proactive.

The AI should remember everything important, predict user needs, reduce manual data entry, coordinate multiple aspects of life, and continuously improve itself through long-term learning.

---

# CORE PILLARS

The product initially revolves around three interconnected systems:

1. Productivity

* Smart scheduling
* Dynamic reminders
* Goal planning
* Daily prioritization
* Adaptive rescheduling
* Context-aware notifications

2. Finance

* Automatic expense capture
* SMS parsing
* UPI transaction tracking
* Location-aware expense prompts
* Spend prediction
* Budget intelligence
* Subscription tracking

3. Health

* Nutrition tracking
* Photo-based food logging
* Voice logging
* Water tracking
* Protein goals
* Habit tracking
* Sleep insights

The user experiences these as ONE intelligent assistant, not three separate apps.

---

# PRODUCT PHILOSOPHY

The AI should always:

* Remember
* Predict
* Suggest
* Remind
* Learn
* Adapt
* Encourage
* Never overwhelm the user

Manual work should approach zero.

Every interaction should reduce future effort.

---

# YOUR TASK

Do NOT build software.

Instead, create a complete documentation roadmap.

Break the company into every document required before engineering begins.

For each document include:

* Purpose
* Why it exists
* Approximate page count
* Sections
* Deliverables
* Dependencies
* Which teams use it
* Completion criteria

Organize everything into phases.

Start with Phase 1 only.

Phase 1 should define the company vision, mission, product philosophy, problem statement, market definition, user personas, success metrics, and guiding principles.

Be extremely detailed.

Assume this documentation will become the foundation for a billion-dollar company.

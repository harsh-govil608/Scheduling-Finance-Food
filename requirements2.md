# ROLE

You are conducting an executive-level design review of the foundational Vision & Mission document for a venture-scale startup.

Assume this document will become the constitutional document for the company and will guide product, engineering, AI, design, hiring, marketing, fundraising, and company culture for the next decade.

You are NOT allowed to rewrite the document for style.

Instead, perform a rigorous architectural review.

---

# REVIEW OBJECTIVE

Determine whether this document is sufficiently complete to serve as the root of every future company decision.

Review it as if you were simultaneously:

* Founder/CEO
* Partner at Sequoia, Andreessen Horowitz, or Accel
* Principal Product Manager
* Distinguished Software Architect
* Principal AI Researcher
* Chief Design Officer
* Chief Security Officer
* Chief Privacy Officer

Challenge assumptions.

Identify missing principles.

Identify future contradictions.

Point out hidden risks.

---

# REVIEW CRITERIA

Evaluate the document across these dimensions:

1. Vision
2. Mission
3. Company Philosophy
4. Product Philosophy
5. User Transformation
6. AI Philosophy
7. Ethical Principles
8. Decision-Making Framework
9. Design Principles
10. Engineering Implications
11. Business Implications
12. Competitive Positioning
13. Category Definition
14. Long-Term Defensibility (Moat)
15. Trust & Privacy
16. Scalability of Vision
17. Internal Consistency
18. Future-Proofing
19. Investor Readiness
20. Founder Clarity

---

# FOR EACH MISSING AREA

Provide:

* Why it matters.
* Risks of omitting it.
* Where it should appear.
* Recommended new section title.
* Detailed outline of what that section should contain.
* Whether it is Critical, High, Medium, or Low priority.

Do NOT write the section itself unless absolutely necessary for illustration.

---

# FINAL OUTPUT

Produce:

## Part 1

Executive Summary

## Part 2

Strengths

## Part 3

Weaknesses

## Part 4

Missing Foundational Concepts

## Part 5

Potential Contradictions

## Part 6

Long-Term Risks

## Part 7

Recommended Additional Sections

Rank them in implementation order.

## Part 8

Coverage Score

Score the document from 0–100 across each review criterion.

## Part 9

Overall Readiness

State whether this document is suitable to become the constitutional document of a billion-dollar AI startup.

If not, explain exactly what is still required before approval.

---

Be demanding. Assume this document will influence thousands of engineering decisions and hundreds of employees over the next ten years. Prefer identifying structural gaps over making stylistic suggestions.

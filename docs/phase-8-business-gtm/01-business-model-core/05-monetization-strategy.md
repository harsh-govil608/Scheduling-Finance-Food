# Document 05: Monetization Strategy

## Document Name
Monetization Strategy

## Purpose
Synthesize the Business Model (Doc 01), Pricing Strategy (Doc 02), Subscription Plans (Doc 03), and Premium Features Business Layer (Doc 04) into one coherent, umbrella revenue strategy — the single narrative that explains how the company makes money end to end, and that is explicitly and permanently reconciled against the Phase 1 anti-dark-pattern and anti-data-monetization principles.

## Why It Exists
Each of the preceding four documents makes a piece of the monetization decision in isolation — value capture mechanism, price methodology, tier structure, gating rationale — but none of them, alone, tells the whole revenue story or proves the pieces are mutually consistent. Without a synthesizing document, it is possible for pricing to be internally sound, tiers to be internally sound, and gating to be internally sound, while the combination still produces a confusing or ethically compromised user-facing experience (e.g., pricing optimized for conversion undermining the trust built by careful gating). This document exists as the final integration checkpoint before monetization moves from strategy into go-to-market execution, and as the artifact investors and the board will actually read to understand "how this company makes money."

## Approximate Page Count
9-12 pages

## Sections
1. **Monetization Narrative Summary** — a one-page, plain-language synthesis of how the company makes money, written for an investor or board audience, drawing directly from Docs 01-04.
2. **Revenue Mix Strategy** — how the various revenue streams identified in Doc 01 (recurring subscription, transactional, B2B2C/partnership) are expected to weight against each other over time, at a directional/strategic level.
3. **Pricing-Tiering-Gating Consistency Check** — an explicit cross-audit confirming that the pricing methodology (Doc 02), tier structure (Doc 03), and feature gating (Doc 04) tell one coherent story rather than three independently optimized ones.
4. **Anti-Dark-Pattern Compliance Statement** — a section-by-section audit of every monetization mechanism (trials, upgrade prompts, cancellation flows, discounting, gating) against the Phase 1 anti-goal ruling out manipulative dark patterns, with explicit pass/fail findings.
5. **Anti-Data-Monetization Compliance Statement** — an explicit, standalone confirmation that no revenue stream in the model relies on selling, licensing, or brokering user data, cross-referenced against the Security/Privacy/Trust framework (Phase 6).
6. **Monetization Roadmap** — the sequencing of monetization maturity over time (e.g., launch with a simple two-tier model, expand to household plans, expand to B2B2C), described directionally without committing to dates or figures.
7. **Trust-Revenue Tension Points** — an honest accounting of the specific places where revenue optimization and user trust could plausibly conflict (e.g., proactive health suggestions that could be perceived as upselling), and the principle the company commits to when that tension arises.
8. **Monetization Risk Assessment** — the key risks to the monetization strategy (price sensitivity, competitor undercutting, regulatory scrutiny of AI-driven financial/health recommendations) and the mitigation posture for each.
9. **Success Metrics for Monetization** — the categories of metric (not target numbers) the company will use to judge whether the monetization strategy is working: conversion rate framework, retention/churn framework, expansion revenue framework, trust/complaint signals.
10. **Governance & Revisit Cadence** — who owns the overall monetization strategy, how it relates to ongoing ownership of Docs 01-04, and the cadence at which the umbrella strategy is formally revisited.

## Deliverables
- A one-page monetization narrative suitable for investor/board distribution.
- A pricing-tiering-gating consistency audit with documented findings.
- A formal anti-dark-pattern compliance statement, section by section.
- A formal anti-data-monetization compliance statement, cross-referenced to Phase 6.
- A directional monetization roadmap.
- A trust-revenue tension log with committed principles for resolution.

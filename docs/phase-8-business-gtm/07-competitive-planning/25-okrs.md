# Document 25: OKRs

## Document Name
OKRs

## Purpose
Define the OKR (Objectives & Key Results) framework and operating cadence that translates the Phase 1 North Star metric and its supporting KPI tree into quarterly and annual company-, team-, and individual-level objectives. This document specifies the mechanism, not the objectives themselves — it is what makes the Success Metrics Document actionable on a recurring operating rhythm rather than a static reference nobody revisits.

## Why It Exists
The Success Metrics Document defines what "good" means once, at a point in time; without a recurring OKR cadence explicitly derived from it, teams drift back toward generic growth and engagement objectives — the exact anti-pattern the Success Metrics Document's guardrail metrics were written to prevent. This document exists to make sure the objectives the company actually manages toward every quarter are re-derived from the North Star metric and its guardrails each cycle, rather than reinvented independently by whichever team is under the most pressure to show progress.

## Approximate Page Count
6-8 pages.

## Sections
1. **OKR Philosophy & Cadence** — the company's operating rhythm (quarterly objectives, annual themes), and how OKRs differ in kind from the KPI tree (KPIs are always-on health metrics; OKRs are time-boxed stretch commitments layered on top of them).
2. **North Star → Company Objectives Traceability** — the explicit mapping method ensuring every company-level Objective set each quarter ties back to moving the North Star metric or a named guardrail from the Success Metrics Document, with no objective admitted that cannot be traced.
3. **Objective-Setting Process** — who proposes objectives, who approves them, the balance of top-down versus bottom-up input, and the timing of this process relative to the Roadmap's review cycle.
4. **Key Result Design Standards** — the rules that make a valid Key Result (measurable, not a restated task, has a stated baseline and target), with a worked example per pillar (Productivity, Finance, Health).
5. **Guardrail-Aware Scoring** — the explicit rule that Key Results may never be built from the anti-metrics named in the Success Metrics Document (e.g., raw engagement or session time cannot appear as a Key Result), and the review-time check that enforces it.
6. **Cascading & Team-Level OKRs** — how company-level Objectives cascade into Product, Engineering, Growth, and Marketing team-level OKRs, and how cross-functional dependencies are handled when a single Key Result requires more than one team.
7. **Scoring, Grading & Retrospective Cadence** — the end-of-quarter grading method, the retrospective process, and how missed Key Results are required to feed into the next cycle's objectives rather than being quietly dropped.
8. **OKR-Roadmap-Metrics Reconciliation** — the standing checkpoint ensuring the OKRs, the Roadmap, and the Success Metrics Document never silently diverge from one another over successive quarters.
9. **Tooling & Visibility** — where OKRs are tracked, who can see company-, team-, and individual-level OKRs, and the transparency norms that apply.

## Deliverables
- Approved OKR framework document.
- OKR template (Objective + Key Results format), seeded with a first quarter's worth of company-level OKRs derived from the North Star metric as a validation pass.
- Guardrail-metric exclusion checklist applied at every OKR-setting and grading cycle.
- Cross-document reconciliation checklist covering OKRs, the Roadmap, and the Success Metrics Document.

## Dependencies
Requires the **Success Metrics Document** (Phase 1) as the direct source of the North Star metric and KPI tree, the **Roadmap** (Phase 8) so OKRs and roadmap sequencing are set on a reconciled cadence, and the **Vision & Mission Document** (Phase 1) so annual themes trace to mission-level priorities.

## Teams
Executive/Founders, Product & Data leadership, all functional team leads (Engineering, Design, Growth, Marketing, Finance)

## Completion Criteria
- [ ] Every company-level Objective in the seeded first quarter explicitly traces to the North Star metric or a named supporting KPI from the Success Metrics Document.
- [ ] At least one Key Result per pillar (Productivity, Finance, Health) drafted as a validation pass, following the Key Result Design Standards.
- [ ] Guardrail-aware scoring rule confirmed to exclude every anti-metric named in the Success Metrics Document.
- [ ] Reconciliation checkpoint scheduled against the Roadmap for the same quarter.
- [ ] Signed off by: CEO (required), Head of Product (required), Head of Data/Analytics (required once hired).

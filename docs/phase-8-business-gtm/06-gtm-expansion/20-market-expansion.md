# Document 20: Market Expansion

## Document Name
Market Expansion

## Purpose
Define the framework and sequencing model for expanding beyond the initial launch market into subsequent markets — the criteria that trigger expansion, the scoring model used to rank candidate next markets, and the repeatable playbook a new market goes through on entry. This document does not name the second, third, or fourth market with fixed dates; it defines the decision system that will be used to choose and sequence them when the time comes, extending the geographic prioritization already established in the Phase 1 Market Definition Document from "which market first" to "which market next, and next after that."

## Why It Exists
Without a deliberate expansion framework, market expansion tends to happen reactively — a founder's personal network, an inbound partnership offer, or a competitor's move in a specific country ends up dictating where the company expands, rather than a consistent evaluation against the same market-attractiveness criteria used to choose the first market. That inconsistency is expensive for a product whose core mechanics (SMS/UPI-style transaction parsing, region-specific financial and cultural norms) mean that some markets are dramatically cheaper to enter than others; entering the wrong market next can require rebuilding parsing and compliance infrastructure that a better-sequenced choice would have reused. This document exists to make market expansion a repeatable, criteria-driven process instead of an opportunistic one, and to make explicit the resourcing and readiness bar a market must clear before expansion capital is committed to it.

## Approximate Page Count
7-9 pages

## Sections
1. **Expansion Trigger Criteria** — the specific performance thresholds (retention, unit economics, market saturation signals) the current market(s) must hit before expansion capital and attention are diverted to a new one, preventing premature over-expansion.
2. **Market Prioritization & Scoring Framework** — an extension of the Market Definition Document's Geographic Prioritization section into a repeatable scoring model (market size, competitive intensity, regulatory complexity, infrastructure/parsing reuse potential) used to rank every candidate next market on the same basis the first market was chosen.
3. **Expansion Sequencing Model** — the stated philosophy for order of expansion (adjacent-market-first for infrastructure reuse vs. leapfrogging to a larger but more different market) and which philosophy this company defaults to and why.
4. **Market Entry Playbook** — the repeatable checklist a new market runs through on entry (regulatory review, localization-readiness check against Phase 2's Localization & Regional Adaptation Experience, Doc 42, partnership sourcing, go-live), so each new market is executed consistently rather than reinvented.
5. **Resourcing Model for Expansion** — the team, budget, and timeline shape required to stand up a new market, scaled by the market's tier under the Localization Strategy (Business Layer) (Phase 8, Doc 22).
6. **Cross-Market Prioritization Conflicts** — how the company arbitrates when two or more candidate markets score similarly and only one can be resourced in a given period, including the named decision-maker.
7. **Regulatory & Compliance Entry Gate** — the mandatory legal/compliance review (data residency, financial services licensing, consumer protection law) a market must clear before entry, coordinated with Phase 6 privacy/compliance work.
8. **Local Competitive Landscape Reassessment** — the requirement to re-run the Market Definition Document's competitive-landscape methodology for each new market rather than assuming the original market's competitive picture transfers.
9. **Expansion Success Metrics & Rollback Criteria** — the metrics that confirm an expansion is working, and the explicit, pre-committed conditions under which the company pauses investment in or exits a market rather than continuing to fund an underperforming expansion indefinitely.

## Deliverables
* Approved Market Expansion Framework document, including the expansion trigger criteria and market scoring model.
* A live-maintained ranked list of candidate next markets, rescored on a defined cadence.
* A standardized Market Entry Playbook checklist reusable for every new market.
* A rollback/exit-criteria policy for underperforming expansions.

## Completion Criteria
- [ ] Expansion trigger criteria are quantified (not qualitative "when it feels ready") and reviewed against current-market performance data before any new market is greenlit.
- [ ] Market scoring model explicitly reuses the Market Definition Document's TAM/SAM/SOM and competitive-landscape methodology rather than introducing an inconsistent new methodology.
- [ ] Market Entry Playbook walked through end-to-end against at least one realistic candidate next market as a dry run.
- [ ] Regulatory/compliance entry gate confirmed with Legal for the candidate market before resourcing commitment.
- [ ] Rollback/exit criteria are pre-committed and quantified before expansion spend begins.
- [ ] Signed off by: CEO (required), Head of Growth/Marketing (required once hired), General Counsel/Compliance Lead (required once hired).

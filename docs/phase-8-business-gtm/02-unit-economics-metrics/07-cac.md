# Document 07: CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)

## Document Name
CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)

## Purpose
Define the methodology for calculating and tracking Customer Acquisition Cost across every channel, segmented by persona, so growth spend can be evaluated against actual payback rather than vanity install numbers. This document specifies the CAC formula, cost inclusion rules, and reporting structure — it does not set final CAC targets, which are derived once real channel performance data exists.

## Why It Exists
A company can grow user count while destroying value if CAC exceeds LTV; without a disciplined CAC methodology tracked per-channel and per-persona, growth spend decisions get made on installs/downloads instead of unit economics. This is especially acute for an AI life-management product where the real cost of a "customer" is not just the marketing spend to acquire them but the onboarding effort required to earn enough trust for the AI to act proactively (Phase 5's Proactivity Ladder) — a channel that produces cheap signups but low trust-progression is not actually cheap. This document exists to make CAC a rigorous, comparable, channel-and-persona-aware metric rather than a single blended number that hides which growth spend is actually working.

## Approximate Page Count
6-8 pages

## Sections
1. **CAC Calculation Methodology** — what costs are included (paid media, referral incentive cost, content/organic amortization, onboarding-support cost) and the calculation formula, built on the shared cost taxonomy from Unit Economics (Doc 06).
2. **Channel-Level CAC Tracking** — how CAC is segmented across acquisition channels (paid social, search, referral, partnerships/B2B2C, organic/content), with the attribution model used for multi-touch journeys.
3. **Persona-Level CAC Variance** — expected CAC differences across the Phase 1 User Personas, and why certain personas are structurally more expensive to acquire (e.g., higher-trust-threshold users who need more onboarding touch).
4. **Blended vs. Fully-Loaded CAC** — the distinction between blended CAC (marketing spend only) and fully-loaded CAC (including onboarding, support, and free-trial cost-to-serve during the pre-conversion window), and when each is reported.
5. **CAC Payback Alignment** — how CAC ties back into the Payback Period Methodology defined in Unit Economics (Doc 06), and the standard time window used for payback calculation.
6. **CAC Trend & Efficiency Monitoring** — how CAC is tracked over time as channels scale (diminishing returns, channel saturation) and the early-warning signals for CAC inflation.
7. **Referral & Organic CAC Treatment** — how zero-direct-spend acquisition (referral, word-of-mouth, organic) is still assigned a non-zero CAC for comparability, and the amortization method used.
8. **CAC Governance & Reporting Cadence** — who owns CAC calculation, how often it is reported, and how it feeds into growth budget allocation decisions.

## Deliverables
- A documented CAC formula with explicit cost-inclusion rules, consistent with Unit Economics (Doc 06).
- A channel-level CAC tracking framework with attribution methodology.
- A persona-level CAC variance model mapped to the Phase 1 User Personas.
- A blended-vs-fully-loaded CAC reporting standard.
- A CAC governance and reporting cadence assignment.

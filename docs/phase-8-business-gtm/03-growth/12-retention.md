# Document 12: Retention

## Document Name
Retention

## Purpose
Define the retention strategy framework — why users keep using the product over months and years — and establish that retention must be generated by genuine, compounding value delivery (the Proactivity Ladder deepening trust and autonomy granted to the AI over time) rather than by engagement-bait mechanics, notification pressure, or guilt-based re-engagement loops.

## Why It Exists
Retention is the growth-loop output most vulnerable to being optimized the wrong way: a team under pressure to move a retention number can always buy short-term lift with re-engagement notifications, streak-shaming, or FOMO messaging, and the Success Metrics Document explicitly names time-in-app and raw notification volume as guardrail/anti-metrics precisely because they reward that shortcut. This document exists to give the company a retention strategy that is structurally incapable of being satisfied by those shortcuts — one where the only way to move the retention number is to make the AI more genuinely useful and more trusted with autonomy, which is the actual mission. It is the document that operationalizes, for growth, the same "encourage, never overwhelm" discipline the Gamification Philosophy Document operationalizes for product mechanics.

## Approximate Page Count
8-10 pages

## Sections
1. **Retention Philosophy** — the company's explicit stance that retention is a lagging indicator of delivered value and trust, not a target to be engineered directly through engagement mechanics, stated as a ruling that governs every tactic in this document.
2. **Retention Loop Position** — where retention sits within the Growth Loops Document's taxonomy as a compounding loop (retained users generate more data, better personalization, more proactive value, deeper retention), and how it differs structurally from acquisition-facing loops like Referral and Virality.
3. **Value-Driven Retention Drivers** — the specific product mechanisms expected to drive retention because they deliver real outcomes (reduced manual effort trending down over time, accurate proactive suggestions, cross-pillar coordination that only a unified assistant can do), each traced back to the Manual Effort Index and Trust & Retention Metrics defined in the Success Metrics Document.
4. **The Proactivity Ladder as a Retention Mechanism** — how retention is expected to deepen specifically because the AI earns more autonomy per user over time (per the Proactivity Ladder Decision Engine), so that a long-tenured user's relationship with the product is qualitatively different (more delegated, less manual) from a new user's, and why this is a defensible, non-gimmick retention driver.
5. **Retention Curve Segmentation** — the reasoning for how retention is expected to differ by cohort, persona (per the User Personas Document), and pillar combination (single-pillar vs. multi-pillar users), and which segment the company treats as the leading indicator of product-market fit.
6. **Anti-Pattern Boundary for Retention Tactics** — an explicit, itemized list of retention tactics ruled out (streak-shaming, guilt-toned win-back messaging, artificial urgency, notification-volume escalation to recover lapsed users), cross-referenced against the Phase 1 Guiding Principles, the Success Metrics Document's guardrail metrics, and the Gamification Philosophy Document.
7. **Churn & Lapse Response Design** — how the product responds when a user's engagement drops (a compassionate, low-pressure re-engagement approach consistent with the Gamification Philosophy Document's Failure and Recovery Experience section) versus the reactivation tactics explicitly ruled out.
8. **Retention Instrumentation & Guardrail Enforcement** — how retention is measured (per the Growth Loops Document's loop health instrumentation model) in a way that is structurally tied to the Manual Effort Index and North Star metric, so a retention initiative that improves time-in-app while making the Manual Effort Index worse is automatically flagged as a guardrail violation rather than reported as a win.
9. **Premium Retention vs. Free Retention** — how retention strategy differs, if at all, between free and premium tiers, and the explicit constraint that compassionate failure-recovery and core proactive value are never withheld from free users as a retention lever (consistent with the Premium Experience Document and the Gamification Philosophy Document).
10. **Long-Term Retention Roadmap** — the directional plan for how retention mechanisms are expected to mature as the Proactivity Ladder matures and as cross-pillar data compounds, sequenced at a strategic level.

## Deliverables
- A written retention philosophy statement ruling out engagement-bait mechanics as a retention lever.
- A retention-driver map tracing each driver back to a specific Success Metrics Document KPI.
- A documented explanation of the Proactivity Ladder as the primary structural retention mechanism.
- A written anti-pattern boundary statement itemized against Phase 1 principles and Success Metrics guardrails.
- A retention instrumentation plan with an explicit guardrail-violation flagging rule.

# Document 11: Virality

## Document Name
Virality

## Purpose
Define which product moments could plausibly generate organic sharing/invitation behavior, and the mechanics that support them, without resorting to manipulative growth tactics ruled out by Phase 1's Guiding Principles.

## Why It Exists
Paid acquisition alone doesn't reach hundreds of millions of users economically; without deliberately designed (but principle-consistent) virality mechanics, growth stays bottlenecked by CAC even if the product itself is excellent. Virality is also the growth loop most tempting to over-engineer with dark patterns — forced contact-list access, fake social proof, spam-inviting — precisely because a small lift in sharing rate has an outsized compounding effect on a viral coefficient. This document exists so the company decides, deliberately and in advance, which sharing moments are genuine byproducts of the product delivering value (worth designing well) versus which sharing mechanics would only work by manipulating the user, and rules the latter out before any engineer builds a share button.

## Approximate Page Count
7-9 pages

## Sections
1. **Native Virality Moments** — product moments where sharing is a natural byproduct (e.g. a genuinely impressive AI insight worth showing someone, a completed goal worth celebrating with someone), not an engineered ask, catalogued per pillar (Productivity, Finance, Health).
2. **K-Factor Model** — the viral coefficient framework and realistic assumptions for this product category, including the structural reasoning for why a proactive daily-use assistant's k-factor dynamics differ from a one-time-use viral utility.
3. **Anti-Pattern Boundary** — explicit list of virality tactics ruled out (fake urgency, spam-inviting contacts, forced contact-list uploads, fabricated social proof, comparison-driven leaderboards) per Phase 1 principles and the Gamification Philosophy Document's banned-mechanic list.
4. **Shareable Artifact Design Principles** — the qualities a shareable output (an insight card, a milestone summary, a recommendation) must have to be worth sharing on its own merit — accurate, flattering to the sharer without misrepresenting data, useful to the recipient — rather than merely eye-catching.
5. **Cross-Pillar Sharing Sensitivity** — rules distinguishing pillars where sharing is naturally low-friction and low-risk (a Productivity milestone) from pillars where sharing carries privacy or social risk (Finance figures, Health metrics), and how the default sharing surface differs accordingly, cross-referenced against Phase 6 privacy commitments.
6. **Invitation & Onboarding Bridge** — how a shared artifact converts a recipient into an activated user without requiring the sharer to have referred them through the formal Referral Document mechanism, and how the two loops (virality and referral) are kept distinct but complementary.
7. **Platform & Channel Considerations** — the reasoning for which sharing channels (messaging apps, social platforms, screenshots) the product optimizes shareable artifacts for, given this product's likely user base and the Market Definition Document's target segments.
8. **Virality Instrumentation** — what must be measured (share rate, share-to-signup conversion, k-factor over time) to know whether a virality mechanic is working, feeding into the Growth Loops Document's loop health instrumentation model.
9. **Experimentation Guardrails** — the constraint that any virality experiment must pass the same Encouragement-vs-Guilt Test used for gamification mechanics before it ships, and who has authority to reject a proposed virality mechanic on principle grounds.

## Deliverables
- A catalogued list of native virality moments per pillar, ranked by expected shareability and sensitivity.
- A k-factor model (structural, not final projected numbers) with stated assumptions.
- A written anti-pattern boundary statement itemized against Phase 1 principles and the Gamification Philosophy Document.
- Shareable-artifact design principles usable directly by Phase 7 design work.
- A virality instrumentation plan feeding the Growth Loops Document's health metrics.

## Dependencies
Requires the Guiding Principles Document (Phase 1), the Growth Loops Document (Phase 8, Document 09), the Referral Document (Phase 8, Document 10), the Gamification Philosophy Document (Phase 2, Document 39), the Market Definition Document (Phase 1), and the Security/Privacy/Trust framework (Phase 6) for cross-pillar sharing sensitivity rules.

## Teams
Growth, Product, Design, Data/Analytics, Trust & Safety

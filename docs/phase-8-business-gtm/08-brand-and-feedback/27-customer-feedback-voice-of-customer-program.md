# Document 27: Customer Feedback & Voice-of-Customer Program

## Document Name
Customer Feedback & Voice-of-Customer Program

## Purpose
Define a structured, proactive program for capturing user sentiment, satisfaction, and feature requests at scale — surveys, NPS/CSAT instrumentation, in-app feedback prompts, and a systematic synthesis process that feeds validated signal into the Roadmap (Phase 8, Doc 24). This document specifies the ongoing, company-initiated listening system, distinct from the reactive, ticket-driven Support function (Phase 8, Doc 15) and from Phase 7's User Testing (a design-research method used to validate specific UX decisions, not a continuous business-wide feedback channel).

## Why It Exists
Support (Doc 15) only hears from users who are already frustrated enough to file a ticket, and User Testing (Phase 7, Doc 30) only hears from the small, recruited sample of users included in a specific research study at a specific moment — neither is designed to represent the sentiment of the full user base on an ongoing basis, and neither is structured to route synthesized signal into product prioritization. For a product whose core promise is proactively managing a user's life, the company has an unusually high obligation to know, continuously and at scale, whether it is actually delivering that value — waiting for complaints or one-off studies means the loudest or most recently-recruited users disproportionately shape the roadmap while the silent majority's experience goes unmeasured. This document exists so voice-of-customer becomes a standing, systematic program with defined instruments, cadence, and a guaranteed path from raw feedback to roadmap input, rather than an ad hoc activity owned by whichever team happens to notice a pattern.

## Approximate Page Count
6-8 pages

## Sections
1. **Program Charter & Scope** — the mandate and boundaries of the voice-of-customer program, explicitly distinguishing it from reactive Support (Doc 15) and one-off User Testing (Phase 7, Doc 30), with a stated principle that this program measures the silent majority, not just the vocal minority.
2. **Feedback Instruments** — the specific mechanisms used to capture signal: NPS surveys, CSAT micro-surveys, in-app contextual feedback prompts, quarterly relationship surveys, app store review monitoring, and social listening, with the rationale for each instrument's cadence and trigger conditions.
3. **Sampling & Fatigue Management** — the rules governing who is surveyed, how often, and how survey fatigue is prevented (frequency caps, randomized sampling, suppression after recent negative experiences), so the program does not degrade the product experience it is trying to protect.
4. **Feature Request Capture & Deduplication** — the structured channel and taxonomy for capturing feature requests specifically (as distinct from general sentiment), including how duplicate or overlapping requests across channels are merged into a single trackable signal.
5. **Synthesis & Analysis Methodology** — how raw feedback (quantitative scores plus open-text/qualitative input) is aggregated, tagged, and analyzed on a recurring cadence, including the qualitative coding framework used to turn free-text feedback into structured themes.
6. **Roadmap Feedback Loop** — the defined, guaranteed process by which synthesized voice-of-customer themes are packaged and delivered into Roadmap (Phase 8, Doc 24) planning cycles, including how feedback-driven requests are weighed against other roadmap inputs (strategy, technical debt, growth bets).
7. **Segment-Level Insight** — how feedback is analyzed by user segment (persona, pillar usage, tenure, plan tier) rather than only in aggregate, so signal from a minority but strategically important segment isn't diluted into an average.
8. **Closing the Loop with Users** — the practice of communicating back to users what changed as a result of their feedback ("you said, we did"), and the guardrails preventing this from becoming a hollow marketing exercise disconnected from actual roadmap outcomes.
9. **Sensitive-Feedback Handling & Handoff** — how feedback that reveals urgent dissatisfaction, safety concerns, or crisis-adjacent disclosures is detected and escalated out of the standard synthesis pipeline to Support (Doc 15) or Trust & Safety, rather than sitting in a quarterly report queue.
10. **Program Governance & Metrics** — ownership of the program, the cadence of reporting (e.g., rolling NPS trend, theme reports per release cycle), and the metrics used to evaluate the program's own health (response rates, time-from-feedback-to-roadmap-decision), consistent with the Phase 1 Success Metrics Document's guardrails against vanity metrics.

## Deliverables
- A documented voice-of-customer program charter with explicit scope boundaries against Support and User Testing.
- An instrument plan covering NPS, CSAT, in-app prompts, and passive channels (app store, social) with defined cadence and sampling rules.
- A qualitative coding framework and synthesis process for turning free-text feedback into structured, prioritizable themes.
- A defined, recurring handoff process feeding synthesized feedback into Roadmap (Doc 24) planning.
- A sensitive-feedback escalation protocol routing urgent signals to Support/Trust & Safety outside the standard reporting cadence.

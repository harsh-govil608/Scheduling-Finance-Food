# Document 12: Night Summary Experience

## Document Name
Night Summary Experience

## Purpose

Define the end-of-day reflection and summary experience — the recap across all three pillars, the tomorrow-prep handoff, and the closing ritual that reinforces the sense that the AI was paying attention all day. This document specifies the anchor moment that closes the day opened by the Morning Experience.

## Why It Exists

Without a deliberately designed closing moment, the day simply stops at whatever the last notification happened to be, and the product loses the single strongest piece of evidence it has that the AI was actually "remembering" all day rather than only reacting in the moment. This document exists so the night summary is designed as one coherent close across Productivity, Finance, and Health — not a Health-app streak screen, a Finance-app spend total, and a Productivity-app task count shown back to back — and so it feeds directly into the next Morning Experience rather than existing as a disconnected daily report.

## Approximate Page Count

6-8 pages.

## Sections

1. **The Purpose of Closing the Day** — the emotional and behavioral function of a night summary, distinct in intent from the morning briefing.
2. **Recap Composition Rules** — the selection logic for what gets included from each pillar; the rule that a summary is a curated recap, not a full log of the day's events.
3. **"The AI Was Watching" Proof Points** — the categories of specific, non-generic detail that demonstrate continuous awareness across the day, drawing on the recall mechanics defined in the Memory Model — Behavioral Perspective document.
4. **Tomorrow-Prep Handoff** — how tonight's summary seeds tomorrow's Morning Experience, and what must carry forward versus what closes out with the day.
5. **Encouragement vs. Judgment** — how misses or failures (a missed budget, a skipped workout, an incomplete task) are reflected without guilt-tripping, directly enforcing the anti-patterns rejected in the Product (Behavioral) Philosophy Document.
6. **Skippable by Design** — the rule that the night summary must remain optional and lightweight, never becoming a second mandatory daily ritual burden on top of the morning briefing.
7. **Weekly/Monthly Roll-up Distinction** — the boundary between the nightly summary and any periodic (weekly/monthly) review, so the two are not designed as duplicates of each other.
8. **Handling a Day With No Notable Events** — the specified low-signal night case, so an uneventful day still closes cleanly without inventing content.
9. **Closing Ritual Format Requirements** — the specification for a scenario matrix and continuity artifact showing how a given night's summary connects to the next morning's briefing.

## Deliverables

* Approved Night Summary Experience document.
* A day-to-day continuity map showing how the night summary's carry-forward items appear in the next Morning Experience.
* An example scenario matrix covering low-signal, high-signal, and miss/failure nights, described narratively rather than as UX copy.

## Dependencies

Requires the **Morning Experience** document (Document 11, the two anchors must connect), the **Daily Flow** document (Document 10), the **Product (Behavioral) Philosophy Document** (tone of voice, anti-patterns), and the **Memory Model — Behavioral Perspective** document (what recall the summary can credibly draw on).

## Which Teams Use This

Product, Design, Content/Conversation Design, Data Science/ML.

## Completion Criteria

- [ ] Recap composition rules have been validated against at least one high-signal and one low-signal day.
- [ ] The tomorrow-prep handoff has been traced end-to-end into at least one Morning Experience scenario and confirmed consistent.
- [ ] At least one miss/failure scenario has been reviewed against the Product (Behavioral) Philosophy Document's anti-patterns list and confirmed compliant.
- [ ] The boundary between nightly summary and weekly/monthly roll-up is stated explicitly and contains no content overlap.
- [ ] Signed off by: Head of Product (required), Head of Design (required).

# Document 9: User Journey Map

## Document Name
User Journey Map

## Purpose

Define the single, longitudinal narrative of a user's relationship with the product — from first install through the end of year one — as one continuous arc that spans all three pillars simultaneously. This document specifies the milestones, trust transitions, and moments of value that every later daily-experience document (Daily Flow, Morning, Night, Dashboard, Notifications, Widgets) must be consistent with.

## Why It Exists

Without a single narrative arc, Productivity, Finance, and Health teams will each draft their own onboarding curve, their own "week one" plan, and their own definition of a "loyal user" — producing three uncoordinated timelines that contradict each other the moment a user touches more than one pillar in the same week. This document exists so the entire company can point to one picture of "what year one with this AI looks like," anchored to the Proactivity Ladder from the Product (Behavioral) Philosophy Document, so that trust and autonomy escalate at the same pace the user actually experiences them, not at whatever pace each pillar team assumes independently.

## Approximate Page Count

8-10 pages.

## Sections

1. **Journey Arc Overview** — the top-level timeline structure (Day 0, Week 1, Month 1, Months 2-3, Month 6, Year 1) used as the shared skeleton for every later milestone discussion, and the rationale for choosing these specific boundaries.
2. **Day Zero: Install to First Value** — what must demonstrably happen across at least one pillar before a user closes the app for the first time, without duplicating the detailed onboarding sequence owned by the Onboarding, Trust & Control documents.
3. **Week One: The Observation Period** — what the user experiences while the AI is predominantly at Proactivity Ladder Level 0-1 (silent observation, passive surfacing), and what "early signs of value" look like in each pillar during this low-trust window.
4. **Month One: The First Suggestions** — when and how the AI's first active suggestions and cross-pillar predictions appear, and how the user's response to them begins to unlock the next rung of the Proactivity Ladder.
5. **Habit Formation Window (Months 2-3)** — how the daily rhythm defined in the Daily Flow document becomes routine, and what "the rhythm has stuck" looks like per pillar and in combination.
6. **Month Six: The Autonomous Trust Milestone** — the narrative moment where higher-trust, pre-filled or autonomous actions become available, tied explicitly to the Proactivity Ladder's higher rungs.
7. **Year One: The Compounding Payoff** — the long-horizon value the AI has accumulated by year one (goal trajectories, financial pattern understanding, health trend understanding) that did not exist at Day 0, and how that is made visible to the user.
8. **Cross-Pillar Moments of Truth** — the small set of high-stakes emotional moments (e.g., a missed budget, a missed workout, and a missed deadline converging in the same week) where the single-assistant experience is tested, and what coherent response is required.
9. **Attrition & Re-engagement Points** — the points on the timeline where disengagement is known or expected to concentrate, and what this document must specify for a win-back response to remain consistent with the rest of the journey.
10. **Journey Map Artifact Format** — the specification for the single horizontal timeline visualization spanning all three pillars, to be produced as a standalone reusable reference.

## Deliverables

* Approved User Journey Map document.
* A single horizontal timeline artifact spanning Day 0 through Year 1, annotated per pillar.
* A tagged list of "moments that matter," each attributed to a pillar (or cross-pillar), for use by pillar-specific product teams.

## Dependencies

Requires the **Vision & Mission Document** (the arc must serve the mission), the **Product (Behavioral) Philosophy Document** (the Proactivity Ladder gates every trust transition on this timeline), the **User Personas Document** (the journey pacing differs by persona), and the **Success Metrics Document** (milestones on this map should be traceable to defined metrics).

## Which Teams Use This

Product, Design, Growth/Lifecycle, Data Science/ML, Customer Research.

## Completion Criteria

- [ ] Every milestone on the timeline (Day 0, Week 1, Month 1, Months 2-3, Month 6, Year 1) has at least one concrete, observable behavior defined per pillar.
- [ ] Every trust transition on the map is explicitly mapped to a rung of the Proactivity Ladder from the Product (Behavioral) Philosophy Document.
- [ ] At least one cross-pillar "moment of truth" scenario has been walked through end-to-end and validated for narrative coherence.
- [ ] Attrition/re-engagement points are cross-checked against the Success Metrics Document's retention definitions.
- [ ] Signed off by: Head of Product (required), Head of Growth (required).

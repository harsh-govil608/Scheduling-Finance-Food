# Document 20: Finance Experience Overview

## Document Name
Finance Experience Overview

## Purpose

Define the overall shape of the Finance pillar as experienced day to day — the home surface, entry points, and trust framing that the Transaction Capture Experience and Budget & Spend Intelligence Experience documents sit underneath. This document establishes the umbrella so the two detailed sub-documents are authored as one coherent experience rather than as disconnected feature specs.

## Why It Exists

Finance is the pillar most likely to feel invasive if poorly framed, because it involves the AI automatically reading a user's SMS messages and transaction history. Without an umbrella document establishing the overall shape, entry points, and trust framing of the Finance experience first, the detailed capture and budgeting documents risk being written as two unrelated feature specs, each solving its own UX problem without a shared sense of what the "money assistant" as a whole should feel like — directly undermining both user trust and the one-assistant mission.

## Approximate Page Count

6-8 pages.

## Sections

1. **Finance Home Surface** — what a user sees when they open into the Finance pillar day to day: the top-level summary, not the transaction ledger detail or budget detail owned by the sub-documents.
2. **Entry Points into Finance** — the structural ways a user arrives at a finance moment (opening the pillar directly, a proactive prompt, a cross-pillar nudge).
3. **Trust Framing for Financial Data** — how the experience communicates, at every relevant touchpoint, what financial data is being read and why, given the sensitivity of automatic capture.
4. **Relationship Between Capture and Intelligence** — the high-level flow from a transaction being captured to it informing budget and spend intelligence, establishing the handoff point between the two child documents.
5. **Financial Tone & Framing Principles** — the emotional register for money conversations (non-judgmental, non-alarming) consistent with Encourage and Never Overwhelm.
6. **Finance Pillar Onboarding Touchpoints (Structural)** — what a first-time user must understand about the Finance pillar before capture begins, at a structural level, with detailed onboarding flow owned elsewhere in Phase 2.
7. **Explicit Non-Scope: Capture Mechanics and Budget Logic** — states plainly that detailed capture, parsing, and correction UX lives in the Transaction Capture Experience Document, and budgeting, prediction, and subscription UX lives in the Budget & Spend Intelligence Experience Document.

## Deliverables

* Approved Finance Experience Overview document.
* A Finance pillar structural map showing the home surface, entry points, and handoff to the two child documents.
* A tone and framing guide for financial communication, reusable by Content/Copy across both child documents.

## Dependencies

Requires the Product Pillars Overview (Finance Pillar Surface capabilities), the Product (Behavioral) Philosophy Document (Phase 1, Encourage/Never Overwhelm), and structural (not detailed-flow) awareness of the Onboarding, Trust & Control documents (Phase 2, Document Group 05).

## Which Teams Use This

Product, Design, Content/Copy, Trust & Safety, Data Science/ML.

## Completion Criteria

- [ ] The Finance Home Surface is defined with no duplication of ledger-level detail owned by the Transaction Capture Experience Document.
- [ ] Trust-framing content has been reviewed and approved by the Trust & Safety Lead as sufficient given automatic SMS/UPI data capture.
- [ ] The handoff point between capture and intelligence has been validated against at least one full worked scenario (transaction captured, budget updated, prediction surfaced).
- [ ] Confirmed no capture-mechanics or budget-logic UX detail is defined here that duplicates either child document.
- [ ] Signed off by: Head of Product (required), Trust & Safety Lead (required).

Expense Capture Pilot ("the app," "we," "us") is a personal finance tracker that reads bank and UPI transaction SMS on your device to log expenses automatically. This policy explains exactly what data the app touches, what stays on your device, and what - if anything - is sent anywhere else.

The short version: your bank/UPI SMS messages are read and processed entirely on your device and are never transmitted anywhere. Only if you choose to use Family Sharing or Smart Split - both optional, off by default - does any of your data leave your device, and even then it's limited to what those specific features need.

1. Data we collect and how it's used

1.1 SMS messages (on-device only)

With your permission, the app reads SMS messages on your device to detect bank and UPI transaction alerts. This happens entirely on your device using local pattern matching - the raw text of your SMS messages is never uploaded, transmitted, or sent to us or anyone else, under any circumstance. Only the information extracted from a genuine transaction message (amount, merchant/payee name, category, date, and direction) is saved, and it's saved in a local database on your device.

Non-financial messages (personal texts, OTPs, promotional messages) are recognized and ignored - they are never stored, in any form, by this app.

If the app's on-device parser can't confidently read a message from a recognized bank/payment sender, and you have an AI-assisted parsing feature enabled, a summary derived from that single message may be sent to our AI provider (see Section 2) to attempt to extract the transaction - never the full SMS inbox, never anything beyond that one message, and only when the on-device parser has already failed.

1.2 Data you enter directly

Budgets, goals, habits, tasks, notes, shopping lists, and manually-added transactions are stored locally on your device only.

1.3 Family Sharing and Smart Split (optional, opt-in)

These are the only features in the app that store data outside your device, and only if you actively create or join a Family or a Smart Split. When you do, the following is stored on our backend (Google Firebase, in the same project this app uses) so it can be shared across the relevant devices:

  • Your display name and phone number (used to identify you to other members)
  • Shared expense/split records you're part of (description, amount, who owes what)
  • Your UPI ID, if you choose to add one for receiving split payments
  • Shared Family module content (tasks, calendar, documents, etc.) you or other members add

This data is visible only to members of the same Family or the same Smart Split, enforced by server-side access rules - not to us for any purpose beyond operating the service, and never to advertisers or data brokers.

1.4 Crash and diagnostic data

We use Firebase Crashlytics to automatically collect crash reports (device model, OS version, app version, and the technical stack trace of what went wrong) so we can fix bugs. Crash reports do not include your financial data, SMS content, or transaction history.

1.5 Profile photo

If you add a profile photo, it's copied into the app's private storage on your device only - it is never uploaded.

2. Third-party services we use

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore database, Crashlytics) - powers Family Sharing, Smart Split, and crash reporting. Google's own privacy policy applies to how they process this on our behalf.
  • OpenRouter - processes AI Assistant questions and AI-assisted parsing (Section 1.1). Only the specific data needed to answer your question or read one message is sent - never your full transaction history or SMS inbox in bulk, and never your raw SMS text.

3. What we don't do

  • We do not sell your data, to anyone, ever.
  • We do not show ads or use your data for advertising.
  • We do not share your data with data brokers.
  • We do not transmit your raw SMS text off your device, under any circumstance.

4. Permissions the app requests

  • READ_SMS / RECEIVE_SMS - to detect and log bank/UPI transaction messages automatically, as described in Section 1.1. You can decline this and still use the app with manual expense entry.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS - to remind you about upcoming bills, budget limits, and subscription renewals you're already tracking in the app.
  • Photo/media access - only when you choose to set a profile photo.

5. Your data, your control

You can delete everything the app has stored on your device at any time from Profile > Clear All My Data and Log Out - this permanently erases your local transaction history, budgets, and settings, and signs you out of Family/Smart Split if you were signed in. Deleting or leaving a Family/Smart Split removes your association with it on our backend as well.

6. Data security

On-device data is stored in the app's private storage, inaccessible to other apps on your device. Data stored on our backend (Family/Smart Split) is protected by access rules that restrict it to members of that specific Family or Split.

7. Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your jurisdiction).
